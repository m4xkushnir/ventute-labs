Kestrel Logistics closed May 2025 with revenue of €720,000, down 6.9% on the previous month. Operating costs were €667,528, giving an operating profit of €52,472. The company ends the month with €2,209,000 in cash and 61 people, with 2 open roles.

This month the bank renewed the overdraft facility, and a large order was delayed to next month. The largest customer accounts for 9% of revenue. A venture fund has offered to lead a seed round.
