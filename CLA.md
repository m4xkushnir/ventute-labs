# Ventute Labs Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0

## How to sign

Post this comment on your pull request, exactly as written:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Then post a second comment with your full legal name and country. Your signature is recorded with your
GitHub username and the date. You sign once, and it covers every pull request after that.

## 2. Definitions

- **Repository**: the GitHub repository `m4xkushnir/ventute-labs`, and any other repository Ventute
  later gives the Contributor access to in writing.
- **Contribution**: any code, script, test, data, prompt, documentation, design or other material the
  Contributor submits to the Repository (by commit, pull request, issue, comment or otherwise) or sends
  to Ventute for use with it, including anything submitted before signing, drafts and work in progress.
- **Confidential Information**: everything Ventute shares with the Contributor that is not already
  public, including the non-public contents of the Repository, product plans and anything Ventute marks
  or describes as confidential. It does not include information that is or becomes public through no
  fault of the Contributor, that the Contributor already lawfully had, or that the Contributor develops
  independently without using Ventute's information.

## 3. Ownership of Contributions

1. **Assignment.** The Contributor assigns to Ventute, with full title and worldwide, all present and
   future copyright and other intellectual property rights in every Contribution, from the moment it is
   created. Where a future right cannot be assigned now, the Contributor agrees to assign it as soon as
   it arises.
2. **Moral rights.** To the extent the law allows, the Contributor waives, and agrees not to assert, any
   moral rights in the Contributions. Where they cannot be waived, the Contributor consents to Ventute
   modifying, combining and publishing the Contributions without naming the Contributor.
3. **Licence as a fallback.** If any right cannot be assigned under the applicable law, the Contributor
   grants Ventute an exclusive (including as against the Contributor), perpetual, irrevocable,
   worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, copy, modify, sublicense and distribute that Contribution for
   any purpose.
4. **Further steps.** The Contributor will sign any further document reasonably needed to confirm
   Ventute's ownership, at Ventute's cost.
5. **Ventute's choice.** Ventute is not obliged to use, merge, publish or keep any Contribution.

## 4. Originality and third-party material

The Contributor confirms that:

1. Each Contribution is their own work, and they have the right to make this assignment. No employer,
   client or school has a claim on it.
2. Any third-party or open-source code, library or data in a Contribution is named in the pull request
   with its licence. Code under licences that would require Ventute to release its own source code
   (such as GPL or AGPL) is not included without Ventute's written approval.
3. AI coding tools may be used, provided the Contributor reviews the output, takes responsibility for it
   as their own Contribution, and does not paste Confidential Information into a tool that keeps or
   trains on it.
4. No Contribution contains malicious code, hidden network calls, install-time scripts, credentials or
   real personal data.

The Contributions are otherwise provided "as is", with no warranty of fitness for any purpose.

## 5. Confidentiality and access

1. The Contributor will keep Confidential Information secret, use it only to make Contributions, and
   not share it with anyone else, during this Agreement and for 3 years after it ends.
2. The Contributor will not copy non-public parts of the Repository to any public location or publish
   them, unless Ventute makes them public first.
3. The Contributor will not try to access any other Ventute system, account, server or data, and will
   not run load, security or automated tests against any Ventute service without written permission.
4. When this Agreement ends, or when Ventute asks, the Contributor will delete their local copies of
   Confidential Information.

## 6. No payment, credit and status

1. **Voluntary and unpaid.** The Contributor contributes voluntarily and will receive no salary, fee,
   equity, royalty or other payment. Neither party has to make or accept any particular amount of work.
2. **Credit.** Ventute will credit the Contributor by name in its release notes for Contributions that
   are merged and used, unless the Contributor asks not to be named.
3. **Portfolio.** The Contributor may describe the Contributions and name Ventute in their CV,
   portfolio and LinkedIn profile, without sharing Confidential Information.
4. **Independent status.** The Contributor is not an employee, partner or agent of Ventute and cannot
   act or make commitments on Ventute's behalf.

## 7. Term and general terms

1. **Term.** This Agreement starts when the Contributor signs it and covers Contributions made before
   that date too. Either party may end it at any time by written notice, including by email, GitHub
   comment or LinkedIn message. Ventute may remove repository access at any time.
2. **What survives.** Clauses 3, 4, 5 and 6.2 to 6.4 continue after this Agreement ends, for every
   Contribution made while it was in force.
3. **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Italy, and the courts of Italy have
   exclusive jurisdiction.
4. **Whole agreement.** This is the entire agreement about the Contributions. Changes must be in
   writing and agreed by both parties. If any clause is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
5. **Electronic signature.** Signing by GitHub comment as described above is the Contributor's
   electronic signature and has the same effect as a handwritten one.
