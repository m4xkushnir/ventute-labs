Harbour & Pine closed February 2024 with revenue of £433,000, up 3.0% on the previous month. Operating costs were £375,257, giving an operating profit of £57,743. The company ends the month with £1,495,000 in cash and 31 people, with 4 open roles.

This month a supplier raised prices by 6%, and a key employee resigned. The largest customer accounts for 18% of revenue.
