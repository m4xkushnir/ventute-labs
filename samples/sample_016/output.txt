Saltmarsh Foods closed September 2024 with revenue of £618,000, up 0.8% on the previous month. Operating costs were £658,738, giving an operating loss of £40,738. The company ends the month with £2,837,000 in cash and 24 people, with 6 open roles.

This month a large order was delayed to next month, and a new product line launched. The largest customer accounts for 28% of revenue. With only 35 people on staff, hiring is the priority.
