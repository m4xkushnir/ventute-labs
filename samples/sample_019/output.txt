Tessera Studio closed August 2024 with revenue of €215,000, up 12.7% on the previous month. Operating costs were €170,255, giving an operating profit of €44,745. The company ends the month with €2,927,000 in cash and 40 people, with 5 open roles.

This month the bank renewed the overdraft facility, and a supplier raised prices by 6%. The largest customer accounts for 25% of revenue. A venture fund has offered to lead a seed round.
