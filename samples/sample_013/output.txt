Café Oriel closed May 2024 with revenue of €769,000, down 5.1% on the previous month. Operating costs were €661,862, giving an operating profit of €107,138. The company ends the month with €659,000 in cash and 55 people, with 4 open roles.

This month a supplier raised prices by 6%, and a new product line launched. The largest customer accounts for 27% of revenue. The board approved a second office in the capital.
