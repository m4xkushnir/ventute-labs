Northgate Clinics closed November 2024 with revenue of 712,000 SEK, down 12.9% on the previous month. Operating costs were 603,331 SEK, giving an operating profit of 108,669 SEK. The company ends the month with 754,000 SEK in cash and 27 people, with 2 open roles.

This month a large order was delayed to next month, and a supplier raised prices by 6%. The largest customer accounts for 38% of revenue, which is a concentration risk worth watching. With only 43 people on staff, hiring is the priority.
