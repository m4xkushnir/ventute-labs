Brightfield Credit closed July 2025 with revenue of €624,000, up 10.9% on the previous month. Operating costs were €472,961, giving an operating profit of €151,039. The company ends the month with €2,401,000 in cash and 87 people, with 6 open roles.

This month a large order was delayed to next month, and a supplier raised prices by 6%. The largest customer accounts for 33% of revenue, which is a concentration risk worth watching.
