Lumen Analytics closed June 2027 with revenue of €852,000, down 12.6% on the previous month. Operating costs were €688,135, giving an operating profit of €163,865. The company ends the month with €2,536,000 in cash and 83 people, with 5 open roles.

This month a new product line launched, and a supplier raised prices by 6%. The largest customer accounts for 34% of revenue, which is a concentration risk worth watching.
