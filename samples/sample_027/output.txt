Panaderia Sol closed July 2024 with revenue of €237,000, up 19.7% on the previous month. Operating costs were €207,162, giving an operating profit of €29,838. The company ends the month with €505,000 in cash and 75 people, with 1 open role.

This month a new product line launched, and a large order was delayed to next month. The largest customer accounts for 41% of revenue, which is a concentration risk worth watching.
