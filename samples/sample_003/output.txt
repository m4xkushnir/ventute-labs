Café Oriel closed August 2027 with revenue of €831,000, down 5.2% on the previous month. Operating costs were €693,400, giving an operating profit of €137,600. The company ends the month with €1,360,000 in cash and 85 people, with 1 open role.

This month a large order was delayed to next month, and a new product line launched. The largest customer accounts for 17% of revenue.
