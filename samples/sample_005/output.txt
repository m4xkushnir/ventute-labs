Kestrel Logistics closed August 2027 with revenue of €593,000, up 15.9% on the previous month. Operating costs were €699,760, giving an operating loss of €106,760. The company ends the month with €2,546,000 in cash and 61 people, with 3 open roles.

This month a key employee resigned, and a large order was delayed to next month. The largest customer accounts for 5% of revenue.
