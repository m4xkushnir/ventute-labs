Tessera Studio closed December 2025 with revenue of €471,000, down 12.4% on the previous month. Operating costs were €568,237, giving an operating loss of €97,237. The company ends the month with €3,241,000 in cash and 67 people, with 1 open role.

This month a large order was delayed to next month, and the bank renewed the overdraft facility. The largest customer accounts for 35% of revenue, which is a concentration risk worth watching.
