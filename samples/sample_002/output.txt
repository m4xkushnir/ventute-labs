Lumen Analytics closed April 2025 with revenue of €752,000, up 15.6% on the previous month. Operating costs were €634,644, giving an operating profit of €117,356. The company ends the month with €1,815,000 in cash and 120 people, with 2 open roles.

This month a large order was delayed to next month, and a supplier raised prices by 6%. The largest customer accounts for 28% of revenue.
