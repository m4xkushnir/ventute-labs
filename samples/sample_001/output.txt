Harbour & Pine closed March 2027 with revenue of £586,000, up 1.1% on the previous month. Operating costs were £552,919, giving an operating profit of £33,081. The company ends the month with £2,696,000 in cash and 48 people, with 5 open roles.

This month a supplier raised prices by 6%, and the bank renewed the overdraft facility. The largest customer accounts for 32% of revenue, which is a concentration risk worth watching.
